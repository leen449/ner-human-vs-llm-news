# Data

Raw datasets are **not stored in this repository**. They keep their original licenses and are downloaded by the notebooks.

| Dataset | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| LLM-generated News (Ayoobi et al., 2024) | https://github.com/navid-aub/News-Dataset | Human-written and GPT-3.5-generated articles |
| CoNLL-2003 (Tjong Kim Sang & De Meulder, 2003) | https://huggingface.co/datasets/eriktks/conll2003 | Training and selecting the annotator |

The silver-labeled dataset produced in this project will be added to `data/silver/` before the final submission.
